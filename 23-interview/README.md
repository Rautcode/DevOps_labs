# 23 · Interview prep (Extra, ~3h)

> **Guided version:** run `bash lab.sh 23` from the repo root. It explains each step, has you type the commands yourself, and checks your hands-on tasks. This page is the reference. **Unlocks after module 11.**

**Goal:** get comfortable explaining your work. Four rounds of spoken questions (Linux and networking, containers and Kubernetes, CI/CD and cloud, SRE and security), a system-design document, a behavioural (STAR) story, and a mock interview exam with live troubleshooting.

## How the questions are scored
You type your answer in your own words. It counts as **strong** when it covers the key ideas (the check looks for the important words). Wrong answers never block you, but each round needs a minimum (for example 5 of 8). After every question you see a **model answer**: read it and compare. Re-take the module to improve your score.

## Structure of a good answer
1. **What it is** in one sentence.
2. **Why it matters** or when you would use it.
3. **A short example** from your own lab work ("in my lab I did X and saw Y").

## The system-design exercise
Cover requirements, the flow from code to production, deployment and rollback, infrastructure as code, monitoring and alerts, secrets, backups, and what you would improve next. Your answer is saved in `playground/design.md`: copy it into your repo as evidence.

## The STAR story
**S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction (what *you* did, step by step), **R**esult (with a number). Reuse a real moment from this lab: the game day, the crash loop, the bad release you rolled back.
